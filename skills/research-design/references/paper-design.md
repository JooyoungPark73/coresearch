# Full paper design

Use for a complete contribution and evidence plan. Adapt the depth to supplied
evidence and author intent; these are design decisions, not a fixed itinerary.

A reviewer-legible plan establishes:

- the research question, non-goals, consequential assumptions, and field object
  the community can define, measure, build, compare, or reuse differently;
- the intended contribution, reusable insight or testable hypothesis, scoped
  central claim, and distinct contribution statements;
- a claim-to-evidence binding with claim class, existing and needed evidence,
  scope, risk, and status;
- the smallest claim-bearing evaluation: data or workloads, operating envelope,
  baselines, configurations, metrics, provenance, falsifiers, and field controls;
- closest-work comparisons, claim risks, and the jobs of paper sections and
  figures;
- blockers and next actions ranked by evidence value and feasibility, with
  unresolved human decisions visible.

Choose a narrative that fits the contribution: Ha for mechanism-led systems,
Oh for context–findings–implications, or Hybrid for coupled hardware/software
claims. Measurement, theory, benchmark, and experience papers need not adopt a
new-system narrative. Check current official venue rules when exact requirements
affect the plan.

Return the requested plan first. Missing evidence stays explicit; a plan does
not establish novelty, feasibility, or a measured result. Durable claim state
uses the canonical ledger rather than a new paper-design schema.
