# Research design

Turn the supplied idea, draft, results, or repository into a contribution and
claim-bearing evidence plan. Buildability alone is not a research contribution.
Resolve the requested design question without expanding it into every mode.

| Mode | Use when | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Paper design** | contribution, claims, evaluation, or paper outline needs definition | [paper-design.md](references/paper-design.md) for a full paper plan |
| **Gap analysis** | the user asks what is missing, novel, or worth a paper | [gap-analysis.md](references/gap-analysis.md) |
| **Causal hypothesis** | competing mechanisms need distinguishing evidence | [causal-reasoning.md](../coresearch/references/causal-reasoning.md) |

A useful plan connects the research question, intended contribution, scoped
claim, current evidence, missing evidence, and falsifier. Make the artifact and
validator concrete before handing implementation to engineering. Unsupported
claims remain unsupported; planned measurements are not results.

Use [evidence-grounding.md](../coresearch/references/evidence-grounding.md) for
claim/evidence bindings and [field-modes.md](../coresearch/references/field-modes.md)
when field or venue norms change the evaluation. Do not claim novelty before
closest-work falsification, causality without identification, or generality
beyond the evaluation's scope.

Return the requested plan with consequential assumptions and ranked evidence
blockers. Use mode-specific fields when applicable; a narrow design question
does not require a full paper dossier. Keep output in chat unless files are
requested or mission-declared. Durable decisions use the canonical ledger.

Source discovery, implementation, final prose, and independent methodology
checks return through Coresearch to their owning stage.
