# Research qualitative

This is an optional, explicit-only method. Turn raw qualitative evidence into
defensible analytical themes without treating frequency as importance or a
vote. Default to in-chat output; write artifacts only when requested or
mission-declared.

## Method

Work in two phases:

1. **Structure:** unitize excerpts with source, session, locator, raw text,
   context, and research question; assign analytical codes; write code memos
   with inclusion, exclusion, examples, and counterexamples; report coverage
   and negative cases.
2. **Synthesize:** consolidate synonyms, split broad codes, categorize by
   mechanism or phenomenon, form themes, test negative cases, relate themes,
   and assess code and meaning saturation. Never claim universal saturation.

A theme is an analytical claim, not a topic label. Preserve representative
quotations with context, group boundaries, alternative interpretations, and
cases that narrow or contradict the theme. Keep analyst interpretation distinct
from participant speech.

## Output

Return the requested analysis plus:

- research question and data coverage;
- codebook with concise memos;
- each theme's analytical claim, supporting codes and excerpts, contexts,
  negative cases, boundaries, and implication;
- relationships, alternative interpretations, saturation limits, and residual
  uncertainty.

Keep material at code or category level when evidence cannot support a theme.
Do not merge participant groups without justification, detach quotations from
context, omit contradictory cases, or generalize beyond the sampled contexts.
When qualitative evidence supports a broader claim, use the shared canonical
evidence and ledger contracts rather than creating a separate research state.
