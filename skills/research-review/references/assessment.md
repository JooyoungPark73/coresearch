# Full venue assessment

Use for a complete readiness assessment or score forecast. Evaluate the paper's
actual claims and evidence, with attention to significance, originality,
technical soundness, reproducibility, clarity, venue fit, and relevant ethics
or limitations.

Give a recommendation with scale provenance, confidence, likely reviewer
variance, and readiness. Support it with:

- a concise account of the claimed contributions;
- strengths and weaknesses tied to claims or evidence items;
- why the evidence warrants this score rather than one level higher or lower;
- acceptance blockers and revisions ranked by likely score impact;
- concrete score-up and score-down conditions, questions for authors, and
  likely decision-level synthesis.

For borderline work, explain both the contribution supporting acceptance and
the evidence gap supporting rejection. Keep incomplete inputs visible in the
confidence judgment. Reception forecasts do not independently verify a claim,
and feasible additions must remain distinct from confirmed results.
