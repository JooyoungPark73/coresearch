# Adversarial evidence audit

Pressure-test external evidence and the agent's own synthesis before a clean
narrative hardens. Skepticism checks evidence; it never substitutes for it.

## Lenses

- **Retrieval bias:** framing terms, venue prominence, missing negative or
  historical work, and adjacent-field vocabulary.
- **Selection and citation bias:** inconsistent inclusion standards, copied
  claims, partial support, or secondary sources replacing primary evidence.
- **Methodology bias:** favorable workloads, configurations, metrics, excluded
  failures, or other applicable design weaknesses.
- **Incentive or funding risk:** identify the incentive, inspect design and
  safeguards, then judge independently. Funding alone is not invalidating.
- **Researcher confirmation:** preferred-method defaults, post-hoc framing, or
  evaluation designed around the desired conclusion.
- **Narrative bias:** suppressed uncertainty, merged weak findings, or
  venue-driven exaggeration.

Apply the same standard to supporting and opposing evidence. Search explicitly
for contradicting evidence before clearing the audit. Record alternative
explanations only when they make testable predictions.

## Output

State the claim and its support; findings by lens; alternative explanations;
**missing counterevidence**; one `skeptic_first_attack`; the **claim that
survives** with confidence and scope; required corrections; and residual risk.

If unresolved bias remains load-bearing, downgrade confidence. For durable
work, update `quality_state.missing_counterevidence`, unsupported claims, and
affected claim states only. Do not discard inconvenient evidence or apply a
stronger standard solely to opposition.
